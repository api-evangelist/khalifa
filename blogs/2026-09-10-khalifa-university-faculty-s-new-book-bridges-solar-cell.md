A new book titled Introduction to Solar Cells, co-authored by Khalifa University’s Dr. Ammar Nayfeh, Associate Professor, Electrical Engineering, and Dr. Sabina Abdul Hadi, Associate Professor of Electrical Engineering at the University of Dubai was published by Wiley in July 2026 to provide students, researchers and engineers with an accessible guide to modern photovoltaic technologies.
